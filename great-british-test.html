The Great British Test – Privacy Policy

Publisher: Brainy Elephant

Last updated: 29 October 2026

About this policy

This privacy policy explains how The Great British Test handles information when you use the app.

Information collected

The Great British Test does not require users to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, precise or approximate location, payment information or other personally identifying information.

When a test is completed, the app sends anonymous test-result information to our database. This consists of:

  • The final test result
  • The number of correct answers
  • The identifiers of the 25 questions presented during the test
  • Whether each answer was correct or incorrect
  • The app version

How this information is used

This anonymous information is used for analytics, including understanding overall test performance and producing aggregate statistics about how users perform on The Great British Test.

The information is not used for advertising, marketing, user profiling or personalised advertising.

Data sharing

We do not sell users' data or share it with other companies or organisations for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

Supabase provides the database infrastructure used to receive and store anonymous test-result information on our behalf.

Security

Information transmitted from The Great British Test to our database is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

Local data

The app may store test progress and results locally on the user's device. Local app data can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Data retention and deletion

Anonymous test-result information may be retained for statistical and analytical purposes. Because this information is not linked to an account or personally identifying information, the app does not currently provide a mechanism for users to identify and request deletion of an individual anonymous result.

Google Play

Google Play may separately process information relating to app purchases, downloads and distribution in accordance with Google's own privacy policies. Brainy Elephant does not receive users' payment-card information through The Great British Test.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
