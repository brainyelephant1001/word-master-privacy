Word Master – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Word Master is provided by Brainy Elephant.

Data collection

Word Master does not collect, store, transmit or share personal user data.

The game does not require users to create an account or sign in.

Game and dictionary data

Word checking and dictionary functions are performed locally on the user's device. Words entered during gameplay are not sent to Brainy Elephant or to third parties.

Advertising

Word Master does not contain advertising and does not use user data for advertising purposes.

Third parties

Word Master does not sell or share personal user data with third parties.

Changes to this policy

If Word Master's data practices change in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, contact Brainy Elephant at brainyelephant1001@gmail.com.
